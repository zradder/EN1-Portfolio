Project 6: Astronaut Tool

Our task was to create a tool that could be used to screw and unscrew bolts in space. It needed to be able to be used with limited mobility (ski gloves), both hands, sideways, and upside down. I used rubber pieces to grip the nut and gears to spin it faster. To change the direction, I coded the motor to reverse its rotation when the device was spun in the pitch plane.
